15 / Before You Buy

The things
you should know.

Does MatricX replace Blender materials?

No. MatricX materials are built on standard Blender shader nodes — they work alongside anything else in your scene.

Do I need UVs?

Not for most materials. Procedural materials work without unwrapping. The fabric weaves are UV-mapped, and trim sheets use UVs by design since they place sheet regions onto faces.

Can I build my own materials?

Yes. Set your own base colour and properties, layer in any of the 15 imperfection types, and save the result as your own custom preset for reuse.

Can I edit materials after applying them?

Yes. Every layer stays editable — reorder, retune, or remove any effect, any time.

Can I control exactly where imperfections appear?

Yes, with Paint Masks — brush any imperfection layer directly onto the mesh instead of relying on a procedural mask alone.

Does it support decals?

Yes — place, project, scale, rotate and recolor decals, plus support for your own decal libraries.

Does it support trim sheets?

Yes — select faces, fit a trim region, and adjust live afterward.

What Blender versions are supported?

Blender 4.5 and newer, tested through Blender 5.2.

What's the difference between Creator Access, Pro Studio, Elite Custom and Enterprise Partner?

All four include the same complete MatricX surface workflow. Higher tiers add faster support, tailored updates, and direct collaboration — not extra core features.

Do I need MatricX Bake?

No. MatricX Bake is for users who need to bake their finished MatricX surfaces into texture maps for workflows such as game engines. If your work stays inside Blender, Pro is complete on its own.
