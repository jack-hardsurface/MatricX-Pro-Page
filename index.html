The idea

Several quiet layers beat
one loud one.

A surface that's been touched, wiped, knocked and left outdoors carries many faint stories — not one strong effect.

MatricX PRO is organised into three workspaces — Materials, Decals and Viewport — and most controls update your object immediately. Build a material once, layer in edge wear, rust and grime with restraint, and it reads as real. No texture painting software, no manual UV work required to get there.

Headline feature — new in 2.0

Paint exactly where
the damage goes.

Every imperfection layer can now be painted directly onto the mesh.

Instead of a procedural mask covering the whole surface, brush rust into the one corner that should have it, or wipe grime off the area a hand would have polished. Paint In starts the mask empty and reveals it in white — flip it to erase from a full mask instead, whichever means less painting for the result you want. Strength softens an overpainted layer without touching a single brush stroke.

Paint masks are vertex-based — MatricX warns you when a mesh is too coarse to hold detail, and offers to add a mask to every object sharing a material.

Custom PBR materials

Bring your own textures.
No nodes required.

Point MatricX at a folder of your own PBR texture set and it does the rest. It scans for Base Color, Normal, Roughness, Metallic, Ambient Occlusion, packed ORM, Height, Emission and Opacity, and turns the set into a reusable material entry stored in your preferences — available in every project from then on.

Custom materials use your authored maps as the complete surface definition — MatricX doesn't composite imperfection layers over work you've already done.

Decals & trim sheets

Decals that project.
Trims that fit.

Decals

Place, project, recolor

Pick a decal, place it on any surface — it aligns to the normal automatically. Reposition, scale, rotate, recolor a label, or project it into final conforming geometry that never prints through to the far side of a closed mesh.

Trims

Fit sheet regions to faces

Select faces in Edit Mode, apply a trim region, and MatricX fits the UVs for you. Automatic, Fill, Preserve or Fit Each Island — plus live Move, Rotate and Scale adjustment afterward.

Add as many of your own decal and trim libraries as you like, from the N-panel or Preferences — MatricX validates every folder and filters out duplicates automatically. One official decal library ships with the add-on; trim sheets are yours to supply.

Live Mapping Compensation

Scale and rotate freely without applying transforms. Live Scale Compensation keeps procedural texture scale visually constant while you resize an object; Live Rotation Compensation keeps edge-based effects aligned as it turns. Both appear automatically when the selected mesh carries a compatible material.

Compensation on vs. off

Two versions of the same scaled object: one with stretched, distorted procedural texture (compensation off), one with the texture holding its scale correctly (compensation on).
