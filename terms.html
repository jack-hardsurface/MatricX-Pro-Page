02The product

MatricX PRO is a Blender add-on. A purchase gives you three downloads:

  • the add-on package (MatricX_PRO_2.1.0_Addon.zip);
  • the same add-on as a Blender Extension (MatricX_PRO_2.1.0_Blender_Extension.zip);
  • the asset pack (MatricX_Assets_2.1.0.zip) with materials, textures, previews and decals.
